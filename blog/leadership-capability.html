Why leadership training needs a capability system

Leadership development becomes more useful when learning goals, practice, assessment, coaching and evidence are connected.

Start with the capability decision

Leadership development becomes more useful when learning goals, practice, assessment, coaching and evidence are connected.

Connect the evidence

Use learning activity as one signal alongside assessment, observation, coaching goals and application evidence. The purpose is to guide the next development action.

Make improvement visible

Define the expected capability, the evidence that demonstrates it and the owner of the next action. Review the evidence over time rather than treating completion as the final outcome.
